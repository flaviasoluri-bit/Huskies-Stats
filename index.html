      📖 Stats Guide — Definitions & Entry Criteria

      This guide defines every stat tracked in the app and tells you exactly when to count it during a game. Share this with the parents tracking stats on game day.

      📊 Parent 1 — Team Stats
      🎯
      Shots For tracked per period
      Any shot taken by the Huskies directed at the opponent's net
      ✅ COUNT when:
      • A Huskies player shoots the puck and it reaches the goalie or crosses the goal line
      • Shot is saved by the goalie (any save = 1 shot)
      • Shot hits the post or crossbar
      • Shot goes in — it's a goal AND a shot
      ❌ DON'T COUNT when:
      • Shot is blocked by a defender before reaching the net
      • Shot misses the net entirely (wide or high)
      🚨
      Shots Against tracked per period
      Any shot taken by the opponent directed at the Huskies net
      ✅ COUNT when:
      • Opponent shoots and our goalie makes a save
      • Opponent shot hits the post or crossbar
      • Opponent scores — it's a goal AND a shot against
      ❌ DON'T COUNT when:
      • Shot is blocked by a Huskies defender before reaching our goalie
      • Shot misses the net entirely
      🥅
      Goals For tracked per period
      Goals scored by the Huskies — entered per period to show when we score
      ✅ COUNT when:
      • The puck fully crosses the opponent's goal line off a Huskies player's stick, skate, or body
      • Tip-ins and deflections count — whichever period the goal happens in
      💡 This matches the score we enter at the top. Track per period so the dashboard shows which period we scored in.
      🚫
      Goals Against tracked per period
      Goals scored by the opponent — entered per period to show when we give up goals
      ✅ COUNT when:
      • Any opponent goal that crosses our goal line — no matter how it went in
      💡 The total Goals Against per period should match the opponent's final score. Goalie Save % is also calculated from this.
      🏒
      Faceoff Wins / Losses tracked per period
      Result of each faceoff taken during the game
      ✅ COUNT a WIN when:
      • A Huskies player wins the puck cleanly off the draw
      • The puck is won back by a Huskies teammate immediately after the draw
      ✅ COUNT a LOSS when:
      • The opponent wins the puck off the draw
      💡 Track every faceoff — zone draws, centre ice, after icing. Faceoff % = Wins ÷ (Wins + Losses). Target: above 50%.
      🟡
      Penalties tracked per period
      Infractions called against the Huskies only
      ✅ COUNT when a Huskies player receives:
      • Any minor penalty (2 min): hooking, tripping, slashing, interference, roughing, holding, high-sticking
      • Any double minor (4 min) — counts as 2
      • Any major penalty (5 min) — counts as 1
      💡 Only track Huskies penalties, not opponent penalties. Target: 2 or fewer per game.
      👤 Parent 2 — Player Stats
      🥅
      Goals
      Scored by a Huskies player
      ✅ COUNT when:
      • The puck fully crosses the opponent's goal line off a Huskies player's stick, skate, or body
      • Tip-in: counts for the player who tipped it in
      • Deflection: counts for the player the puck last touched before going in
      💡 Only 1 goal per play, to 1 player. Also count +1 Shot For on the same play.
      🤝
      Assists
      Pass(es) leading directly to a Huskies goal
      ✅ COUNT when:
      • Up to 2 assists per goal — the last player to pass the puck to the scorer, and the player before that
      • Primary assist: the player who passed directly to the scorer
      • Secondary assist: the player who passed to the primary assister
      ❌ DON'T COUNT when:
      • The scorer picked up a loose puck with no clear pass
      • There was an opponent touch between the pass and the goal
      💡 Max 2 assists per goal. Some goals have 0 or 1 assist — that's fine.
      🏒
      Passes
      Successful passes completed by a Huskies player to a teammate
      ⚠️ Only count passes when the Huskies are on offense — when they have possession in the offensive zone or neutral zone moving forward.
      ✅ COUNT when:
      • The Huskies have the puck and are attacking — pass is received cleanly by a teammate
      • A pass in the offensive zone or on an offensive rush in the neutral zone
      • A pass that leads to a shot or a goal also counts as a pass
      ❌ DON'T COUNT when:
      • The Huskies are defending in their own zone — defensive clears and breakout passes do not count
      • The pass is intercepted by an opponent before reaching a teammate
      • The puck is rimmed around the boards without a clear intended recipient
      💡 Assists are a subset of passes — a pass that leads directly to a goal gets both a Pass and an Assist credit.
      🧤
      Goalie — Saves & Save %
      Tracked separately in the Goalie Stats card
      ✅ COUNT a Save when:
      • The goalie stops a shot that would otherwise have gone in
      • Any stop on goal counts — glove, pad, stick, blocker
      Save % formula:
      Save % = Saves ÷ Goals Against × 100
      💡 Goals Against is pulled automatically from the opponent score at the top. Target: Save % above 85%.

      💡 General Tips for Parents
      • When in doubt, don't count it — it's better to under-count than over-count.
      • Focus on your assigned stats only — Parent 1 on team counters, Parent 2 on player names.
      • Counters update live on both phones — you'll see each other's inputs in real time.
      • Use the printed tally sheets as backup if internet is unavailable at the rink.
      • After the game, tap Record Game to History before closing the app.
